Singlark

A private voice coach in your pocket. Find your real range, sing in tune and build your voice with a short workout every day.

What it does

  • Voice check-up: your lowest and highest notes, how well you match a pitch, and the longest note you can hold. Repeat it to see your progress.
  • Daily workout: a few minutes of warm-ups and exercises planned around your range.
  • Live pitch graph: see your voice against the target note as you sing.
  • Hear your progress: each check-up keeps a short clip, so you can compare your first one with today.
  • Gentle on your voice: Singlark warns you when you push too high, too loud or too long.

Private by design

Your voice is analysed on your phone and never uploaded. No account, no ads, no analytics. Read the privacy policy.

Free and Plus

The check-up and free practice are free, with three daily workouts to try. Singlark Plus unlocks unlimited workouts, your full history and charts, and every progress clip, monthly or yearly with a free week.
