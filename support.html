Common questions

Singlark doesn't hear me. Check that the microphone is allowed in your phone's Settings → Singlark. Sing a little closer to the phone, in a quiet room.

The note jumps around. Sing a steady “ah” at a comfortable volume. Headphones help, because the app then doesn't hear its own reference notes.

My throat feels tired. Stop and rest. Singlark is a practice tool, not medical advice. If hoarseness or pain lasts more than two weeks, see a doctor or a voice specialist.

Restore my subscription. Open Settings in the app and tap Restore purchases, signed in with the same Apple ID or Google account you used to subscribe.

Cancel my subscription. On iPhone: Settings → your name → Subscriptions. On Android: Google Play → Payments & subscriptions. You keep Plus until the end of the period you paid for.

Delete my data. In the app: Settings → Delete all my data. Nothing is stored anywhere else.
